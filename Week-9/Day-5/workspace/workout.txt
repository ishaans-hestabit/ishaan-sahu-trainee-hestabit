# Modified 2-Day Workout Plan
## Day 1 (Upper Body)
1. **Warm-Up**: 10-15 minutes of light cardio and dynamic stretching
2. **Push-Ups**: 3 sets of 12 reps
3. **Incline Push-Ups**: 3 sets of 12 reps
4. **Tricep Dips**: 3 sets of 12 reps
5. **Overhead Tricep Extension**: 3 sets of 12 reps
6. **Pull-Ups**: 3 sets of as many reps as possible
7. **Bodyweight Rows**: 3 sets of 12 reps
8. **Bicep Curls**: 3 sets of 12 reps
9. **Hammer Curls**: 3 sets of 12 reps
10. **Cool Down**: Static stretches for chest, triceps, back, and biceps
## Day 2 (Lower Body and Core)
1. **Warm-Up**: 10-15 minutes of light cardio and dynamic stretching
2. **Squats**: 3 sets of 12 reps
3. **Lunges**: 3 sets of 12 reps (per leg)
4. **Calf Raises**: 3 sets of 12 reps
5. **Glute Bridges**: 3 sets of 12 reps
6. **Planks**: 3 sets, holding for 30-60 seconds
7. **Russian Twists**: 3 sets of 12 reps
8. **Leg Raises**: 3 sets of 12 reps
9. **Shoulder Press**: 3 sets of 12 reps
10. **Lateral Raises**: 3 sets of 12 reps
11. **Cool Down**: Static stretches for legs, shoulders, and abs
Additional Tips:
- Hydration: Drink at least 8 glasses of water per day
- Nutrition: Focus on a balanced diet with plenty of proteins, complex carbohydrates, and healthy fats
- Sleep: Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night to aid in recovery
